Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-19

Notch Pals (“the app”) is designed so that your information stays on your Mac.

What the app does with your information

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code, and it does not use the network.

What is stored on your Mac

Stored locally in the app’s own sandbox container, never sent anywhere:

  • your tasks and reminders, notes and sticky notes, timer state and settings;
  • your buddy choice, name, and its “closeness” level;
  • references to files you drop on the Shelf (the app remembers where they are; it does not copy or upload them).

Kept in memory only and discarded when the app quits (never written to disk):

  • your last five clipboard items, but only if you turn Clipboard on. Items marked private by the app that created them (for example password managers) are never recorded.

Permissions

The app asks only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Calendars: to show your upcoming events, warn you before meetings, and add events you create. Events are read on your Mac and are not copied elsewhere.
  • Notifications: to show reminders and timer alerts.
  • Files you drop or choose: so the Shelf can find your files again.

It does not use your camera, microphone recordings, location, contacts or photos. To let your buddy react to a video call it only notices whether a microphone is in use while a call app is running; it never records or listens to audio.

Children

The app contains no content aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your control

You can turn off any feature in Settings and remove your data inside the app at any time: delete tasks, notes and sticky notes, clear completed tasks, and use Clear all on the Shelf and in Clipboard (clipboard items also disappear when the app quits). Permissions can be changed in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Deleting the app does not necessarily delete its data: macOS can keep the app’s sandbox container after the app is removed. To delete everything the app stored, quit it, then in Finder choose Go > Go to Folder, enter ~/Library/Containers/app.notchpals.mac and move that folder to the Trash (Finder may show the folder as “Notch Pals”).
